Kemist helps you look up a medicine's NAFDAC registration record and find licensed pharmacies, clinics, and hospitals — all in one place.

Launch Kemist

Check a record

Type the NAFDAC registration number from the pack, or the medicine name, to see the official registration record.

Find a pharmacy

See licensed pharmacies, clinics, hospitals and labs near you, with directions and opening hours.

Report a problem

Tell us about a medicine that looks fake, is packaged wrongly, or has a wrong record. Reports are reviewed by people.

What a check can and cannot tell you

A match means NAFDAC has a registration record with that number. It does not prove the pack in your hand is genuine, because fake packs can copy real numbers. Always check the pack, buy from licensed pharmacies, and ask a pharmacist when unsure. Kemist is not medical advice.
