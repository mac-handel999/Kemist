What a check can and cannot tell you

A match means NAFDAC has a registration record with that number. It does not prove the pack in your hand is genuine, because fake packs can copy real numbers. Always check the pack, buy from licensed pharmacies, and ask a pharmacist when unsure. Kemist is not medical advice.
